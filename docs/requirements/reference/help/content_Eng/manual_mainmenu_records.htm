High score


Here you can view your game stats and compare them to the best achievements. Aside from the overall sum of points of a particular player the stats show such rates as game end date, flight rank, number of destroyed ships, number of star systems liberated from dominators, skills obtained, etc.





To get detailed information on a particular record:

Click the required line in the table. All the information will be shown on the right side of the screen.

To delete your own score:

Press 'Clear' at the bottom of the screen.


If you win, you will be able to partake in the Galactic Ranger Rating. You will find a special button with an image of an envelope next to your score. If you press it, you will create a special file, which you can send to our web site. After that your name will be enrolled on the list of Galaxy Liberators forever! More details at www.rangers.ru
