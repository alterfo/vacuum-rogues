Space diseases

The friendship between the races have both positive and negative sides. One of the negative sides are the diseases of other humanoid forms. The diseases can influence the skills, reducing or increasing them for the period of the illness. In this section you can get to know the most dangerous ones.

Note. Further the change of skills is displayed as: reduction —, growth +


Blindness
The beginning stage that leads to a complete loss of one's eyesight. Blurred vision of targets on the radar and in the immediate outside space.
— — — —


Chekumash
Presence of surreal objects in the space: delirium planets, nebulae. Extramundane voices calling the infected one.
no affect


Holy Fanaticism
In open space all ships seem to be dominators. Everything on every planet looks like it's Dominator's. Temporary outbreaks of rage, the folks around lose all respect for an infected humanoid. Charisma drops to the minimum. Whilst accuracy, maneuverability, and leadership skills are increasing.
+ + — +


Complex Immunicide
The immune system of the infected becomes weaker. As a result he is becoming an easier target for viruses. Any contact with other races is unwelcome.
no affect


Mysterious Luatanza
The player cannot effectively shoot, demand money or extort cargoes, maximally improves the relationships. From time to time the Supporting fund of luatanza cases sends a message, and the sick one receives money afterwards.
— +


Substance Addiction
The sick becomes addicted to stimulants. The limbs are shaking, the equipment wear grows. Attack and maneuverability skills are minimal.
— — —


Whirlwind Concussion
Happens in a battle. The sick experiences the sleep disturbance. As a result the sick can't concentrate, loses his accuracy and maneuverability skills. The trading skill disappears completely.
— — —


Muscle distension
Severe pain is obstructing your movements, hampering your concentration and the ability to give orders, what results in the loss of almost all the skills.
— — — — —


The Grand Malossossus
A sick maloq starts rapidly gaining weight, as well as his weight in the society. The changes are so significant that even the weight of the ship becomes considerably higher, which effectively decreases its speed. At that the leadership and trade skills are growing by a order of magnitude.
+ +


Bitter Pelenosha
A disease widespread among peleng children, it also plagues some part of peleng grownups. The sick has problems when dealing with the equipment, which reduces its operation time.
—


Aka Seziyanka
The sick loses the concentration, greatly misses in battle and loses the equipment in space. It's interesting that the slowdown of brain reactions has a positive effect on the feyan trading skills.
— — +


New Molizone
It is strictly prohibited to sell food or medications on all planets (this is considered as smuggler operations). The hormonal exchange intensifies, which leads to the increase of accuracy and maneuverability skills.
+ +


X-ray sickness
Develops under the influence of the high radiation. The sick experiences the decline of mental activity, which results in the drop of aptitude for learning. The sick person recovers in one or two months, depending on the radiation dose. The healing methods are still unknown. Appears during the activation of the Transfactor beacon. During the repeated activation of the Transfactor beacon before the recovery, the negative effect increases.
no affect



The word 'disease' is perceived as something bad. That's not always the case in the game. The disease simultaneously brings both the positive and negative effects. Look at the change of your skills carefully, evaluate the advantages and disadvantages of suffering a disease at this moment of the game. If a disease brings benefits, you can neglect to cure it in the Medical Center.


Stimulants

Stimulants are recommended only for the cases of emergency, since they build up a tolerance. You will receive a variety of stimulants offered at any medical station. Your pilot rank and the game difficulty level determine the amount of stimulants offered, as well as the amount which you can accept. A rookie can use simultaneously use one or two stimulants. Correspondingly a Commodore can use a variety of stimulants. When you buy one stimulant, you lose an opportunity to use it repeatedly till its lifetime expires. The lifetime of all stimulants depends on the game difficulty level.

Note. In the Medical Center you can purchase a medical insurance. The medical centers provide discounts for their services within the period of insurance lifetime.


The Maloq Sizha
A liquid stimulant. Use once a year only, otherwise it builds up a tolerance. It improves the accuracy and the maneuverability almost to the maximum. As the saying goes about this stimulant: "Rarely, but right on target".
+ +


One-eyed Khamas
A peleng stimulant. In 3227 it was adopted for other races. Looks like a small one-eyed creature. The creature sticks to a humanoid, roots itself into his brain and provides him with the power of hypnosis. All ships, no matter whom the pilot takes the toll from, give it termless. The stimulant is bad influence on the reputation, reduces the Leadership and Charisma.
— —


Stardust
A stimulant in form of micropowder taken from the particles located next to the black holes. Ingest only. Improves the brain functions of any race representative. Increases a number of skills.
+ + + +


Super Technician
A feyan bio-stimulant that increases technical skills. The equipment wear almost stops. The effect is extremely limited.
+


Time Gaalistra
The gaalian combat stimulant. Recommended for use during the battles. Its short-time reaction makes the brain work by thousands times quicker. The ship's speed and the fire accuracy drastically grow.
+ +


Blood Djogar
A solid oily clot of maloq sand Djogar. The stimulant improves the blood supply, replacing the red corpuscles with its own more powerful artificial substitutes. They make the organism less susceptible to diseases.
no affect


Ragobam Whisper
The stimulant is prepared of a stinky swamp water elixir, with radioactive squeeze made of Ragobam toad poison. Increases the skills of explanation and persuasion, improves the attitude to a pilot. After that you can eat Ragobam toads.
+


Shakhmandoo Leader
The effect of this peleng substance provides you for some short time with self-belief, a precious quality, which helps to close the ranks of team-mates around you! The stimulant improves the Leadership and allows to conclude the partner contracts for long terms.
+ +


Psychotropic Cache
Under the effect of the stimulant the brain sends impulses to the radar and scanner, tremendously increasing their power. A scanner-man however irritates those around him, which results in the negative attitude to the actions of the latter.
—


Trademark
A temporary hypodermic trademark. In fact it is a sign of a vast trading experience. Thanks to the stimulant you can sell your equipment at full price.
+


Doubleplex
A short-lived virus artificially developed on the Earth in 2117, as part of the relativity theory experiments. The rumor has it that anyone who is infected with that virus is always successful in business, although the fact has never been proven. As a side effect the virus causes slight weakness, which results in attack and maneuverability decrease. However the virus host reliably receives a high reward for the governmental missions, since superstitious humanoids are available at each planet.
— —


Absolute Status
It is visible only during the bio-scanning in governmental institutions. Its owner witnesses special minimal requirements. And namely - milder terms when fulfilling the governmental missions.
+



The use of stimulants can help to earn more money at the initial stage of the game, save money on the repairs or increase the killing power of the weapons. The use of "Blood Djogar" allows to significantly reduce the chance of infecting with various diseases.
