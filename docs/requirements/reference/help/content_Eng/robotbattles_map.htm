Maps of planetary battles


In total there are 74 maps of planetary battles in Space Rangers HD: A War Apart.
You can receive the majority of battles at once after the liberation of a system.


If you've either failed to win a planetary battle, or wish to fight the one you liked once again, or the authorities didn't offer it to you during the game, you can start it at any time beyond the game process. Press R key in the main menu or a special button located at the bottom of the window to launch a list of all the planetary battles. Having chosen the battle (here below you will find a list of all battle names), you trigger it. After the battle, you will return to the main menu.

Agro-Intestinal Refinery

Difficulty: medium

We urgently need the help of an experienced strategist, ranger!
Our planet has become an object of interest for the dominators due to its enormous mineral resources. Several months ago each robot type managed to land a self-extracting base here.

Don't use one and the same method on all the maps. Take into account the circumstances, which build on the entire map, and not only next to your area or your troops. For example, it is useful to take your time and watch the fighting between two of your enemies, waiting till they make each other weak and then backstab them both. Keep a constant watch on the resource accumulation, since their abundance is the chief factor of the victory. The capture of the enemy's resource plants reduces the amount of robots produced. Actively use the terrain relief for your manuevering. Build the robots with the similar movement speed, so they could reach the target simultaneously opening the fire at the same time. It will bring your the concentration of forces and the advantage on the direction of your strike. A part of robots in your group should be repairable - it drastically increases the life of your forces and saves your resources. Use the manual robot control, for example head for the advancing enemy. At the distance of the artillery blow start moving back and deliver a strike. It allows to significantly weaken the enemy's offensive. Don't forget to summon reinforcements from time to time. Set up the defense of your plants and your base, taking into account the location of turrets and the terrain relief.
