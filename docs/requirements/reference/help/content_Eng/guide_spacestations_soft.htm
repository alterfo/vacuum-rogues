To have a chance to use the anti-dominator software, first you should install Intercom, the special decoding software. Then you will be able to affect the dominators in course of the communication with them. You can receive the software from the government as an award for the liberation of a system or for the completion of a mission, and on the military bases as a reward for the struggle against the dominators. It is possible to buy software on pirate bases, but you should have a nod-account in the Ranger Center. You can use one copy of the software only one time, therefore you can receive several copies at once.


Using program helps the player to simplify the hostilities against dominators. For example they decrease the kill power of dominators' weapons. The software is a good aid when liberating the systems occupied by dominators. Using programs gives a chance to obtain unconventional analogs of the standard equipment.

Software against dominators

 Intercom

This software is a decoder of dominators' speech. It helps to dialogue with them and download other antidominator programs. When your success in the area of dominator elimination reaches the ears of the Command of the Coalition Military Forces, you will receive first several copies of Intercom (up to 20 pieces). As soon as they are over — you will get more of them. You can purchase this software for nods on the pirate base.



 Alarm signal

Having received an alarm signal, the dominator will throw away a part of the equipment and weapon. The dominator throws away up to three units of equipment or weapon. If you use Alarm signal against one and the same dominator, being deprived of weapon and defense systems, he become a sitting duck. But he never throws away the engine and the last gun. Having stayed with a signal pistol or a torpedo launcher, he will start running from you, attacking from afar. Available for nods at a Pirate base.



 W-Coder

A universal codec for hacking the weapon system passwords. It will take a dominator some time to restore the data. Available for nods at a Pirate base.



 Gatrix-matrix

A huge matrix of unsorted data overloads the robot’s electronic chips with a stream of data. The robot would fly aimlessly and shoot chaotically. Available for nods at a Pirate base.



 Shocker-5300

The program disturbs the operation of dominators' electrical circuits, obstructs the navigation. The robot starts flying slowly and shooting poorly. Available for nods at a Pirate base.



 Absolute weapon

Zero-level order for self-destruction. Dominators obey inviolately. Available for nods at a Pirate base.



 System blackout

All robot data gets deleted. Dominator shuts down and don't react any longer. Even the droid doesn't operate. Available for nods at a Pirate base.

Software against bosses

 Counteraction

Program of logical negation for the contact with the software center of blazeroids. Actually no software is needed for the contact, however with the help of Counteraction you can destroy or neutralize the Blazeroid with minimal efforts. You can purchase this software at the Scientific base, when the blazeroid department of the SB finishes its researches.



 Energotron

Software development of a model for conversion of energy into matter. It is interesting for terronoids. You can purchase this software at the Scientific base, when the terronoid section of the SB finishes its researches. It helps to easily get rid of the Terron.



 Dematerializer

In the area of the closest visibility the kelleroids don't have a chance for teleportation and materialization. You can purchase this software at the Scientific base, when the kelleroid section of the SB finishes its researches. It helps to destroy the Keller in a hyper-space battler with more ease.



 Submodem

Opens the brain of the tested one for the data upload providing an access to the system of the transmitter. Available only at the Pirate Base. In a talk to Terron the Submodem allows to disable his weapons, (which simplifies the battle in the space), to land on the planet, (which is needed to defeat the Terron in a ground battle) or disable the breeding of terronoids, (which allows to eliminate them with ease).
