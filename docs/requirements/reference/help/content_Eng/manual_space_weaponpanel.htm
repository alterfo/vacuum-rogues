Weapon panel (W)


Press 'Show/Hide weapon' to open the weapon panel, which consists of 5 slots, each of them can house one weapon. As a result the ship can have up to 5 guns, all of them are simultaneously battle-ready, if the ship's hull allows it. The weapon panel serves for targeting the weapons (enemy ships, asteroids). You can aim several guns at one target, as well as aim various guns at various targets. The weapons lying in the cargo bay, can't shoot, however you can mount them and prepare for the battle.


To mount a gun from the cargo bay into the weapon slot:
1. Press 'Ship' (S).
2. Click the weapon in the slot. The mouse cursor will take the shape of a hand holding the weapon.
3. Click the weapon slot, in which you wish to mount the weapon.

To close the weapon panel:
Press 'Show/Hide weapon' one more time.
