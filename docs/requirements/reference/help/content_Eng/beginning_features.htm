Features and improvements of Space Rangers 2


• "Space Rangers 2: Dominators" (further - SR2) represent a unique combination of many widely differing game genres. The stardust of space odysseys and adventures, the tactics of planetary battles and the strategy of liberation of enemy-invaded space systems - all this is available in the game. Even the fans of arcade battles will find something interesting for themselves. It's hard to imagine another game, which has more facets than this one. Space Rangers 2 is simply unbeatable!

• In the universe of SR2 everything is interrelated, it is a large and incredibly interesting world, which can live and develop on its own, without your participation! There are more than 60 star systems, totalling to more than 250 planets and stations, inhabited by reasonable beings, and many other space objects, which welcome to interact in process of the game.

• Aside from you in the world of SR2 live their lives hundreds of space ships, controlled by AI pilots. They roam from one system to another, trade, fight, purchase new equipment and weapons - in short, they are taking care of their business, completely independent on the player. These are peaceful and military ships, pirates' and dominators' ships, additionally there are 50 rangers, which are your direct competitors in the deed of galaxy liberation from the looming menace.

• You can choose, which of the 5 different races, populating the Galaxy, you will represent, and use the unique capabilities of this race to achieve your goals.

• It is possible just to live in the world of SR2! You are free and can do anything, you like: complete quests, trade, take part in planetary battles, fight against the dominators - or to become a pirate in the systems of the Coalition.

• Some of the players may like being promoted up the ranking scale for enemy ships destroyed, while others will find it more exciting to collect micromodules and gain experience that allows them to upgrade the 6 pilot skills.

• It is possible to complete the game in different ways: you can hunt down and destroy the Dominator bosses, or you can try and make a deal with them. Therefore the world of SR2 will suit the tastes both of fans of tough battles and convinced pacifists.

• We have implemented a unique dialogue system. All ships can communicate with each other: advise on lucrative trading routes, extort "road tolls", ask for help, offer military alliances or simply trade with each other.

• The vast number of standard equipment types and artifacts with unique properties will allow you to make your ship equipment as diverse as possible.

• The game features 15 weapon types, let alone the modifications, which the player can use in the tactical and arcade battles.

• The pep of SR2 would rightfully be the text quests, that the player will receive on different planets throughout the game. A total of 56 thrilling text quests, from fairly easy to completely brain-wrecking ones completing which the player will not only be able to obtain valuable artifacts, micromodules, and Dominator-combating software, but - most importantly - the true fun.

• More than 20 excellent musical pieces will allow you to totally dive into the atmospheric world of the game.

• And lots, and lots more there in SR2, you name it!


Key novelties compared to the previous release:

• Entirely new graphics: navigation panel, menu, ships, backgrounds of uninhabited planet, equipment.

• The planetary battles in full 3D! A total of 45 planetary maps with their own flora and fauna.

• Exploration of uninhabited planets with the help of probes.

• Two new space stations: medical base, and business-center.

• A possibility to buy any space ship of the Coalition: pirate, peaceful, military.

• Each hull depending on the series, is provided with its own amount of slots for equipment and boost support.

• Weapons are divided into types: fragmentation, energy, missile.

• A flexible system of equipment upgrading with the help of micromodules.

• 13 alien diseases of the Hero and 12 stimulants for temporary upgrade of the Hero's skills.

• The opportunity of investing into the construction of space stations.

• A new parameter in the ranger rating, which replaced the good old points - experience, which grows thanks to successful actions of the player.

• An opportunity to order military operations and partake in them together with the military base.

• Completely upgraded hangar. An opportunity to scan ships, travelling with the player around the orbit.

• The navigation system modernized - now a commonly used double-click with a left mouse button is used.

• An opportunity to personalize your game character with the help of more than 90 avatars.

• Prices for goods can be reviewed without landing on a planet.

• 3 new sectors appeared on the galaxy maps.

• Dynamic movies, which create an unmatched atmosphere of the ranger world.

• The whole world of SR2 has grew to become wider and more convenient for a full-scale life in it.
