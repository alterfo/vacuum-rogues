Ranks in the pirate fleet



If a ranger accepted a 'special offer' on the pirate base and became a pirate, it will open an opportunity for him to make an alternative carrier. Each 'pirate' action results in pirate points, after a particular sum of the points the player will be able to receive a new pirate rank (provided he has fulfilled a special mission). After getting a new rank the counter will be set to zero. Points for the elimination of dominators and liberation of star systems are provided for the combat experience gained.

The amount of points required for a new rank
Fresh meat 0
Ship boy +100
Raider +250
Shipmaster +450
Cut-throat +750
Chieftain +1000
Khan +1500
Baron +3000

Amount of points for pirating
Robbing a peaceful ship 1   Robbing a dominator Shtip 1
Destruction of a peaceful ship 8   Destruction of a dominator Menok 2
Destruction of a military ship 16   Destruction of a dominator Smersh 4
Destruction of a ranger (not pirate) 24   Destruction of a dominator Urgant 8
Elimination of a business center or a scientific base 24   Destruction of an Equentor dominator 16
Elimination of a ranger center or a military base 32   Liberation of a system from dominators 16
For each 3000 cr of pure profit from contraband 1   Destruction of a Flagship 60
Destruction of a dominator Klig 1   Destruction of a dominator Bertor 24

Noob

A fresh pirate. He is still on the righteous path, but already hesitating.


Ship boy

Inexperienced young pirate, he is at the beginning of his carrier. He has to prove that he has landed the pirate syndicate not for nothing, and has opportunities of the carrier growth.


Raider

An ordinary pirate, which went through a certain school of hard knocks and crime. He poses a danger for the space trade. Such criminals can boast an impressive collection of unpaid fines for minor offenses.


Skipper

Officer among outlaws and outlaw among law-abiding citizens. Impossible to rectify. Prison for such pirates is a homeland, but mostly because their authority helps to maintain order in prison cells.


Cutthroat

An experienced pirate, professional. Unsuitable for taking captive: award is small, problems are large. In the detention centers these dudes are a pain in the neck for the administration, when easily eliminate the moles among the prison-mates.


Capo

A special category of pirates. They rarely get into prison, as they either have already been sentenced to death in absentia, or they are that dodgy, so the space police is fooled all the time trying to capture them.


Khan

Enjoy an enormous respect in the criminal world. They claim for the highest pirate title. The most desirable trophy during the anti-pirate raids, since they possess the secret knowledge about the secret pirate bases and agent network. Therefore each earnest khan must kill himself facing the danger of being arrested

Baron

The highest title in the pirate hierarchy. That's all about it.
