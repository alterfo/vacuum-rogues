Modifications




Modifications represent a special type of ship`s weapon, initially developed by pirates, however later copied by the forces of the Coalition. The main typical characteristic of such equipment is that it is constructed on the ground of already existing samples of armament, but with the help of Akrin technologies weapon gains earlier unprecedented properties. Due to the need of a more precise targeting, the modifications actively use onboard scanner, that is why for successful operation of the modification the scanner’s capacity should exceed the capacity of the enemy’s defense generator and this fact should be given a special attention. When the scanner’s capacity allows breaking through the defence field of a target, a special sign appears by the weapons’ icons, showing that the effect of modification will be applied to the target chosen.





There are seven types of modifications with the details as follows below.



• Diphrode - Serial set of standard devices and program improvements increasing the damage of the weapons of different types, which had appeared yet before the start of the war with Dominators. The common factory set of «Diphrode» includes the details of refitting of any type weapon: missile, energy and fragmentation. The sets are produced in a big amount and weapon modernized is available at the board of a peaceful liner and turret of the Coalition’s fighter, as well as in the arsenal of a famous pirate.

• Liphocarus - Variants of these systems are applied in the armed forces of the Coalitions, as well as by the thugs of the pirate’s brotherhood. The mode of shooting with serial shots into a chosen area of the hull gives additional “beyond armor” penetration effect to the weapon of «Liphocarus», modification, as a shot following the first one, hits the structure of the ship before the armor restores. The greatest effect is applying at heavy damaged targets.

• Armoloc - Despite exterior differences of the ships of different types, some approaches in their arrangement are similar. They are the same in the spacecrafts of the Coalitions and the pirate’s units and even in Dominator’s space robots, which perceived maloq’s approach in the spacecraft architecture for a known reason. This peculiarity gave the military men of the Coalitions a thought of special weapon, hitting enemy’s ships in their most tender spots. One of them is an external concentrator of the channels for weapon control. After re-equipment of the targeting complex and regulations of the shooting rate the fragmented and energy weapon of «Armoloc» type can disable the concentrator with precise shots. The rival appears to be unable to make the return fire till the concentrator unit is repaired.

• Javitor - A repair droid, operating in the open space at external armored panels of the hull was primarily vulnerable for targeting shooting. Yet from the times of Klisan war the constructors of the Coalition have started to complicate the algorithm of droids’ operation, training them for additional artillery-resistant maneuvers, which increased the chances of robots to survive the shootings of Klisans. Now, in connection with non-announced civil war between the Coalition and pirates, more tricky means are applied to achieve the victory. The both sides are striving to create the weapon hitting even the most crafty droids. However, after the use of the existing modification of «Javitor», weapon, droid more often stops operation for a short period of time, and then occurs absolutely unharmed.

• Adropter -During the centuries after the end of Klisans war all the Coalition’s races have kept on improving ship equipment. But non-standard equipment, even that produced since XXX century, is still in a limited production. Nevertheless since recently there have been reports about individual units of «Adropter», armament, produced by the pirates and operating on the principle of a Blast wave Localizer. The Pirate’s technology is far not perfect, but it enhances the probability of preservation of valuable trophies after destruction of the enemy’s ship.

• Stylermain - the origin of this pirate’s know-how is still a question. Maybe the structure of some energy and fragmented weapons of «Stylermain» Stylermain modification includes the devices of cyber-electronic fighting, similar to those used to broadcast anti-dominators programs. Anyway, it happened so that during fights between the ships of the Coalition and pirate’s ships that a spacecraft, hit by a precise shot of the pirate, dropped a part of cargo from its cargo bay. In appearance the effect resembles the revelation of Fei disease "Aka Seziyanka" with only difference that the goods are falling down from the ship under attack and not from the infected one.

• Inveiter - – In the very end of 3299 patrol squadron of the Coalition, using its quantitative advantage, destroyed the personal ship of one of the pirate’s captains. The patrol captured unharmed equipment from the hit ship, including the armament of all three types in earlier known modification «Inveiter». During the analysis of the trophy, the engineers defined that the equipment of the main guns and computer modules of missiles were refitted with jammers for a standard engine ignition system. Till this moment the ignition system was considered absolutely protected from electromagnetic attacks, but now it is not excluded that the Coalition following the pirates shall put into service similar means and get down to improvement of protection of engines in its ships. Now this armament constitutes a risk of damaging of an engine for those facing its owner. One should remember that each side of the conflict uses its weapon modifications. Thus for example, the modifications Diphrode and Armoloc are produced only by the forces of the Coalition, however individual representatives of the pirate’s clan can somehow capture these samples of equipment. The modifications of Adropter, Stylermain and Inveiter on the opposite can be purchased only from shops, controlled by the pirates. The modifications of Liphocarus and Javitor are common for the both parties to the conflict. It’s also noteworthy that different modifications can fit better for definite situations, but not for constant use.
