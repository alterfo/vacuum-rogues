Space stations


Unlike planets, space stations do not go around the stars, but are permanently located in a certain point in the space (although in case of an enemy attack they can return fire and even fly). When you hover the cursor over a space station a pop-up window with additional information about it appears. Having landed on a space station, you can do the same what you did on the planet, i.e. trade, buy equipment and arms or visit the information center. At that each station has its distinguishing features.

There are 6 types of space stations:
• Ranger centers
• Scientific bases
• Military bases
• Pirate bases
• Medical bases
• Business centers



Ranger centers

• Collect nodes and manufacture micromodules.
• Present one micromodule to rangers for the New Year.
• Maintain ranger classification.
• Inform all planets about the activity of other rangers.


Scientific bases

• Carry out anti-dominator researches.
• Manufacture research probes for uninhabited planets
• Repair nonstandard equipment.
• Can upgrade standard items, but only once.
• Diagnose and repair ships.


Military bases

• Track the combat activities of rangers and try to coordinate their efforts with the actions of the military.
• Inform rangers about ongoing hostilities.
• Assign pilot ranks to rangers, give precious gifts.
• Cell to rangers the best weapons.
• Diagnose and repair ships.
• Provide rangers with anti-dominator software.
• Carry out military operations a la carte.


Pirate bases

• Buy and sell any goods, including contraband.
• Provide a wide selection of second-hand equipment.
• For good money can fake documents and even change your race. For a pirate-ranger this is the best way to restore his reputation in the eyes of the governments of different planets.
• Sell anti-dominator software, camouflage.
• Diagnose and repair ships. Big discounts for pirate-rangers!


Medical bases

• Carry out medical examination and heal the discovered diseases.
• Sell powerful stimulants of interim action on the bio-organisms.


Business centers

• Provide public loans. Carry out lotteries with valuable gifts among loaners.
• Collect investments for different projects.
• Carry out researches aimed at trade optimization.
• Insure life of pilots.


Dominions

• Available for visiting only when player sticks to the pirate clan.
• Jumps to other systems to relocate or attack.
• Chase down militaries while they are on a mission.
• Able to redirect ships towards them during their hyperspace jumps for robbery.
• Grants pirate licenses.
• Upgrades equipment.
• Able to construct pirate ships.
• Informs player about war theater status.
• And even does mini-jumps inside one system!



In order to make new bases appear you should liberate the entire sector of dominators.
If you change the trajectory of your flights after the liberation of the last system in the sector occupied by dominators, it can change the order of space station appearance.
Building space stations allows to increase the chance to repel the attacks of dominators on the free systems. The endurance of space stations grows after the modernization.


In the game the player has an option to modernize the space stations. Choose in the government building of any space station an option: 'I wish to participate...'. For a small sum of money you can start the modernization. You can repair, change and add equipment, apply micromodules and artifacts, upgrade the efficiency of the skills. All these are carried out in the same way as in the player's spaceship. The only difference - is that all you wish to mount during the modernization should be initially placed into the storage. The efficiency of the skills grows thanks to the free experience points of the player.
