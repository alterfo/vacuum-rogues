Micromodules


The micromodules allow to change the characteristics of the standard and dominator equipment. The micromodules are divided into levels: there micromodules of level 1, level 2 and level 3. The maximal change of equipment characteristics provide the micromodules of level 1, the minimal - level 3. Some micromodules can be incompatible with the equipment of particular races. The equipment of dominators allows the installation of any micromodules. One can purchase the micromodules in the Ranger Centers, discover during the exploration of uninhabited planets, receive as a reward for the completion of a government mission or as a trophy during some hostilities.


Mounting a micromodule into equipment:
1. Click a micromodule in order to take it. The mouse cursor will change into a hand, holding a micromodule, the slots, where you can mount the micromodule into will get highlighted.
2. Drag the micromodule into the highlighted slot of your choice and click a mouse button.

Note. Impossible to mount a micromodule into the equipment, which already houses a micromodule for the second time, in addition you can't dismount a micromodule.

You should decide, what is more profitable for you, insert a micromodule or upgrade the characteristics of a research station equipment. For instance, if you don't have money for the upgrades of the scientific station , the installation of a micromodule saves time required for earning money. The installation of a micromodule sometimes will be more profitable for the characteristics and and the cost of the equipment, with further decrease of repair costs. You can return the micromodules, which you didn't need into the Ranger Center in exchange for a compensation on your nod-account.
