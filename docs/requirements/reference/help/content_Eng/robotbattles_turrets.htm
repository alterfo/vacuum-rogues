Building turrets


Bases or plants can be equipped with defenseive structures - turrets. A turret should be built only in a place especially designed for it, one building can have up to 4 of such places.

When a base or plant is selected, you can see a 'Building turrets' button on the window in the right bottom corner. There is also information about the places available for building activities.



Note. If the 'Building turrets' button is inactive, it means that this building has no more place for turrets at the moment and you can't build anything new there until one of the turrets belonging to this building, is destroyed.

Press the 'Building turrets' button to display the available variants of turrets (some of them can be inactive, it means that they are lacking resources). The mouse cursor will turn into a turret type chosen by you, as soon as you have its icon clicked. The places for turrets will be selected on the map with green color.



The cursor in form of a turret will be highlighted in red until you move it to the place reserved for this turret. If you wish to cancel the building of a turret, press the Cancel button in the right bottom corner.
