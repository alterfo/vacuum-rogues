Strategic Control Mode


There are two ways to contol a robot: In the strategic mode and in the manual control mode.



Click a robot. A green circle will appear around it, which means that this robot is selected. The robot control buttons will be displayed in the bottom right corner of the screen.



This panel is divided into three parts:
1. The selected robot and its hit point.
2. Robot'a weapon and the Manual control mode button.
3. Command buttons.

System of commands:
There are five command buttons in the upper part of the screen with the direct indication of the goal:
• stop
• go
• patrol
• attack
• capture
(additionally for special types of units - repair and explode)

In the bottom part of the screen there are three command buttons with automatic action:
• destroy enemy objects - your robot engages its intellectual block and starts searching and destroying robots, turrets and buildings on its own;
• capture buildings - a robot will automatically find buildings on the map and start capturing them;
• defend base - a robot defends the base from the enemy invasion.

The unicity of the commands with automatic action is that you won't need to issue orders to your robot all the time, it will be sufficient to press a button one time, and your robot will act on its own.

Hint. The game offers a convenient feature of group selection of robots. Hold down the left mouse button and draw the selection frame on the screen to select all your robots in the visible area. You can easily unite them - press Ctrl or any figure from 0 to 9, when a group of robots is selected. From now on the pressing of this button will result in the selectin of the entire group.
