Navigation panel


The navigation panel is located at the very bottom of the screen and is comprised of the following elements.


On the left there is a System menu button. Further, there may be a random number of various hint icons: - current tasks, - saved dialogue screens, - hints, etc. Further to the right there is a current date of the game world, free space available on the spaceship and the amount of player's money. Finally, there is the foremost button of the game on the right - "End of turn".

There are always seven buttons over the navigation panel: "Show weapon panel", "Ship scanning", "Talk to ship", "Flight log", "Spaceship", "Ranger rating" and "Galaxy map". Besides, depending on the current game mode several additional buttons appear over the navigation panel (see Planet mode, Space mode).

When the player hovers the cursor over one of the interface elements, a short description of this element appears on the panel and the respective hot key, provided there is one.


Saved information, icon -

There is a special element in many dialogue windows - . This is a very useful option!


If you press such an icon, you will save the contents of the current window in the memory of the board PC, be it prices for goods in the Trading center or news in the Information portal. With that, a new icon appears on the navigation panel . Later you will be able to open or delete any saved, if you don't need it any longer.

To save the contents of the dialogue window:
Press the icon , located in the dialogue window. The icon connected to this window will appear on the navigation panel.

To view the contents of a previously saved window:
Hover over the mouse cursor over the icon on the navigation panel.

To delete a previously saved dialogue window:
Right-click the relevant icon on the navigation panel.

To delete all previously saved windows, hint icons, icons of the completed quests:
Press the key F12.

To delete all previously saved prices:
Press the key F11.


Note. The operation of other hint icons is similar to the work with the icon . The difference is that they appear on the navigation panel themselves, and not as a result of user's actions. Some important hints are impossible to delete, until some certain game events happen (e.g., till the term of the current governmental task has expired).
