Planetary quests


A text quest is a living book, you can not only read it, but also influence the events happening inside it. A part of the quests has been created by the Union of Independent Quest-Writers.

There are two groups of text quests: math and atmospheric. Math quests urge to solve some task or a logic puzzle. The atmospheric quests invite you to dive into the life of another planet and help its inhabitants in some way.


If you either failed to complete a quest, or you wish to replay the one you liked, or the government officials haven't offered it to you in the government building during the game, you can launch it at any time beyond the gaming process. Press the Q key in the main menu or a special button, located at the bottom, to launch a window with a list of all quests. Selecting a quest (here below you can see a list of them), you start it. After the end you will return to the main menu.
