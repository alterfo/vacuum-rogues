Scanning (I)


If your spaceship is equipped with a scanner, you can try and research the interior of some other ship, i.e. see the equipment and weapons mounted inside it. This information will be very useful in a battle, since you will have an image of your opponent's power.

To scan a ship:

Option 1
If the option 'Right-click a ship' is set by default in the game settings you will be able to easily scan another ship with a right click of the mouse.

Option 2
1. Press 'Scanning' (I). At that the cursor will change its appearance into .
2. Click a ship, which you wish to scan. If your scanner is stronger than the defense field of another ship, your will see the interior of this ship.


Reasons of scanner failures:

1. If the radar is out of order or the selected object is out of the radar range, the scanner will fail to work.

2. The dominator ships don't get scanned because of their structure.

3. In order to scan a ship it is required to overcome its defense field. If its power surpasses the power of your scanner, the scanning will fail. (The power of the defense field of any ship is displayed, when you hover the mouse cursor over this ship.)
