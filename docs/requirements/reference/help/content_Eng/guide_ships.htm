Ships


All ships in the game are controlled by AI-pilots, which have their own world view and consider themselves individuals. They try to analyze the deeds of other pilots and take their own steps. The latter strongly depend on pilot's race and the personality type. Maloq pilots are usually fearless, peleng ones - aggressive, gaalian and human - diplomatic, feyan - cowardly.

There are the following types of ships:
• Rangers
• Peaceful ships
• Military ships
• Pirates
• Dominators
• Unknown ships

Each type is represented by a number of models, there are 40 of them in total in the game.

The main business of rangers is to liberate the star systems that have been invaded by the dominators. They sleep and dream to find and destroy the dominator bosses - Terron, Keller and Blazer However not all of them are true to high ideals. Some of them entered the ranger ranks for the sake of profit. Such individuals behave immorally: rob other ships, deal with the contraband...

AI-rangers in the game have high intellect; they can do everything, what the player can do, except completing the government missions. Rangers can be hired by the player or by other rangers.

The behavior of rangers is unpredictable, as their nature can change in course of the game. Today a ranger is heroically fighting against the dominators, and tomorrow he abandons the fight and deals with cargo transporting, in order to save money for repairs and ship's upgrade.

The peaceful ships live their own life. These are transport, leisure and diplomatic ships. They are worried about their own business, tourism or negotiations. As a rule, peaceful ships have poor defense and are constantly attacked by pirates.

The pilots of the peaceful ships usually try to make a compromise. They unconditionally pay the so-called 'road toll' to the pirates. They never pursue their enemies. Even if they defeat their enemies, they agree for the minimal ransom. Though there are exceptions. Once a pilot bears malice against a ranger, and shoot at him at each encounter. :-)

The military ships are on duty defending their own planets and submit only to their own governments. They immediately enter the orbit when the ships of dominators or of other enemies appear. They are usually decently armed and attack in groups. The military angry with you are not to be trifled with. If you are unsure in your strength, it's better to negotiate with them and pay tolls.

Pirates live off of pillage and plundering. They are only interested in their own personal profit. For the sake of profit they attack their wounded fellow-pirates. Favorite pirate's occupation is to collect 'road toll' from the ships flying by. Who refuses to pay will be attacked at once. Therefore a fast-flying ship or powerful, weapons would help to escape unpleasant encounters with the rogues.

The most bloodthirsty pirates don't usually live long, since outraged governments offer rewards for their liquidation. The best professionals hunt for them in order to destroy.



Dominators

There are several types of dominator ships, varying in size, speed and firepower. It is known that they seldom travel alone: sometimes up to two dozens of their ships come to capture one single star system. The dominator ships are controlled by a super-powerful PC.

Scanning Dominator ships is possible only with the help of scanners manufactured by dominators. At that you can scan only the ships, which series correspond with the scanner series.


Also notice, that ships of "Klig" type can be encountered only in one case: if the "Bertor" ship is located nearby. This is the only dominator type, capable of reproducing "Klig" - class ships in space. Dominator-controlled planets are not manufacturing them for unknown reason. "Klig", in spite of its decent parameters, may be really dangerous, because indeed this dominator can ram his enemies and deal significant damage. "Bertor" is a huge armored dominator-production facility. He is also equipped with portable high frequency field generator, with different effect for various series. For example, in range of blazeroid "Bertor" all allied dominators will have bonus damage, terronoid one will provide bonus repair and a kelloroid will give a defense boost. Although "Kligs" are relatively cheap in production, their big brothers cost a lot to dominators, and that’s why there are only a few of them in the Universe. They also have a large escort then flying between star systems, that protect them from possible attacks. There are also rumors, that these types of ships are immune to anti-dominator programs, but this is still unclear.



Pirate Clan

Besides dominators the player will have to deal with the organized resistance of pirates, which, in addition to ordinary pirates, who switched from the Coalition side, include especially trained warriors with unique pirate ships, designed for the complete elimination of all enemies, both metallic and organic.

Unknown ships

    

Sometimes you would encounter ships of unknown races. The highest chance of encounter is in the black holes. No one knows where they come from or what they are after. However the victory over them can bring the hero some valuable arifact.
