Buildings


There are two types of structures on the map:

1. Bases are structures, where you can build robots.



You can easily distinguish the bases from other structures - they have extendible doors with an elevator, which deliver a manufactured robot up.

When you click such a plant, the right part of the screen will display the buttons of the plant functions: Build turrets and robots.

2. Plants are buildings for production of resources: titan, micromodules, energy and plasma.

     Titan plant Engineering facilities (micromodules)

   Electric plant (energy)   Plasma lab
A click on a certain object selects it and allows to use the available functions, for example, if you select a base, you can build robots or turrets. When you select a plant you can build turrets in the places especially designed for each structure.


Types of turrets:

Light gun
is a double-barrel gun. Fires two barrels simultaneously with low-yield projectiles.

Heavy gun
is a single-barrel gun. Fires from a long barrel with a powerful explosive shell.

Laser
is a stationary laser jet. Fires with a continuous laser beam. Burns through all types of armor.

Missile launcher
is a powerful missile weapon. Fires armor-piercing missiles with plasma engines.
