Landing

To land on a planet or a space station:

Option 1
Double-click the left mouse button to indicate the object for landing, and your ship will fly to that place immediately.

Option 2
1. Click a planet or a station you wish to land on. The display will show the trajectory of your ship's flight to the destination, and the cursor will take the shape of a planet. If the cursor has the shape of a ship, click the destination one more time.
2. Press the 'End of turn' button. Your ship will fly to the destination, and later will land on it.

Note: The ship automatically calculates only the approximate amount of days till the landing.
