New features of Space Rangers HD: A War Apart



Improvements of the game comparing to the previous title release:

• Additional big storyline.

• Upgraded AI of the enemies. Now they can use the boost and unique equipment.

• A new side in the conflict, which advocates its own interests.

• Over 20 new text quests.

• Over 30 new maps for planetary battles.

• Over 50 new standard governmental quests.

• Over 20 new musical compositions.

• New unique equipment.

• New game mode: space quests.

• Large amount of new game elements, surprises and secrets.

• Upgraded graphics supporting high resolutions of the display.

• Support of modern hardware and up-to-date operation systems.

• Added mode of advanced adjustment.

• Adds new weapons and artifacts.

• Weapon modifications with unique properties.

• Improved behavior of the artificial intelligence: now computer pilots can use afterburner and choose different combat strategies, and pirates can gather and attack from pirate bases, and in case of the attack failure, fly away from the system.

• The game now supports 3-rd party mods.

• The function MAKEDUMP was added, which is the toolkit of game saves modifications.
