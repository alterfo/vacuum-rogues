Chase


There are 3 chase modes. To activate any of them, proceed as follows:

1. Click a ship you wish to follow one or several times (make a small interval between the clicks, otherwise the system percieves it as a double click and the ship will automatically launch this chase mode). Toggle the modes by clicking the mouse. The first click activates the mode 'Close in', the second - 'Chase', the third - 'Autocombat'.

2. Press the 'End of turn' button. Your shipp will follow another ship in the selected mode.

Each mode has its own cursor.


Description of the modes:

• Close in - follow the selected ship as a convoy.
• Chase - keep at the distance of a shot from the alien ship.
• Autocombat - catch the enemy and fight him to the bitter end.


Note. Advantages and disadvatages of the autocombat:

Advantages:
• no need to issue orders at each turn;
• player's ship flies itself and fights.

Disadvantages:
• The player can't control his ship till the enemy dies, or till the player's ship is severely damaged, or till the player has the 'End of turn' button pressed.
