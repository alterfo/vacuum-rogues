Talk (T)


You can enter into a conversation with the caption of another ship. His answers depend on the current conversation. If it is his trade ship, he will inform you about his plans: where he is heading to, where he is coming from and what is the level of prices there. If it's a pirate or a ranger, depending on his relation to the player he can give a useful piece of advice offer you to mind your own business and hit the road.

Use dialogues to make a military alliance or ask someone to cease the attack. You can trade with another ship or you can intimidate someone and take him money or a part of the cargo. You can hire rangers with a preliminary discussion of the terms.
If you succeeded in hiring a ranger below the radar you will see an additional panel of partners. Press the ship icon with the left mouse button to focus the camera on it; press the right mouse button to start a dialogue with the partner. You can give him orders and he will obey you! For instance, if you wish to land, you can address your subordinate and order him to land He will immediately change the course. If your subordinate has stayed behind, don't worry, he will find you himself, no matter where you fly to. If he is in some other star system, his icon on the panel is inactive.


To enter a talk with another ship:
1. Press 'Talk' (T). The mouse cursor will change its shape into .
2. Click a spaceship you wish to talk to.


To quite a dialogue with another ship:
• Choose 'End of talk' or press "Close window".

The communication is possible only with the ships located in the range of the radar operation. So, if the radar is broken or isn't mounted, talking to other ships is impossible.

Note. In the talk mode you can move the space map either on the radar, or directly in the space, holding down the right mouse button. In addition, ships and planets, which are currently the subject of discussion, have in the dialogue window the "Center camera" button; .


The ships can themselves start communicating with the player or each other. The icons of intercepted messages are located at the bottom of the navigation panel and can be reviewed by the player.
