Hull types


Hulls are divided into series. Each series is a standard hull modified depending on the quality and expensiveness of the series: slots are added or removed, size is increased or decreased.


For the sake of economy take into account when buying a hull which purposes you need it for. If you wish to conduct the hostilities, it's recommended to have a hull with open slots for a repair droid and a defense shield generator. If you wish to complete government missions, it's recommended to equip a hull with a boost. Take into account that in the future you may mount micromodules on it for the activation of the closed slots and upgrade of the hull features. The presence of open slots for artifacts is highly important.
