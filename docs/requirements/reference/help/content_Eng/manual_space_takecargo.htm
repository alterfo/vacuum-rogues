Picking up items


An item is anything that a ship can carry in its cargo bay. It is either equipment or weapons, goods or artifacts, quest or storyline items. Any item from the space can be picked up aboard with a gripper of the relevant power. If a mouse cursor turns into a hand when you hover over an item, it means that you can pick this item up.

Attention! You won't be able to pick up items without a gripper, look it up in the equipment store.

To pick an item up aboard:

Option 1
Double-click an item. A ship will close in and pick an item up with the gripper.
Hint. If you plan to pick several items up, click each of them one time (a small icon in form of a hand) then double click the last item. The ship itself will fly to pick up the items.

Option 2
1. Approach an item so that it is in the area of the gripper's range (the range is indicated with a circle when you hover the mouse cursor over an item).
2. Click an item. An icon in form of a small hand appears next to it.
3. Press the 'End of turn' button. The item will be picked up by the gripper aboard.

In order to 'select' all items in the gripper's range press the Num 0 key. To select all items in the system press Num . key. The icons in form of a hand will appear around the items, which can be picked up.



Note. An item won't be picked up aboard in one of the following cases:
• It is out of the gripper's range. In this case close in to the item and make another attempt.
• The power of your gripper is insufficient for such a big item. You should acquire a more powerful gripper.

A situation is possible, when several ships at the same time wish to pick up one and the same item. In this case the one located as close as possible to the item will have it.

Any item located in the space you can shoot down from your guns - thus your enemies won't get their hands upon it.

Note! If your ship is overloaded, it won't fly till you get rid of the overload. Hence take only the cargo, you can carry and fly with.
