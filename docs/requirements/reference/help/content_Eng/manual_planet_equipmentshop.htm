Equipment store (E)


In the equipment store you can find equipment and weapons for sale. The level of the equipment in the store depends on the type of the race and its technological progress. In its turn the price of each item is determined basing on its parameters, size and quality. The smaller is the item, the higher is its quality, the higher is its cost.



Remember! The most qualitative items are of gaalian origin, the second in quality are the fayean ones. The items of the human origin are of medium quality, the peleng ones have lower quality comparing to them. The maloq things are the worst: they got quickly broken and require constant maintenance. Despite of it they are the most inexpensive, and affordable from the economical point of view.


Each item in the store has its own marking in form of horizontal rectangles in the right part of the icon, Roman numbers over the icon and letters at the bottom. All of these signs indicate one and the same parameter - the level of technological development of the item. There are in total eight technological levels for the equipment (A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H).




To find out the characteristics of a certain piece of equipment:
hover the mouse over the relevant equipment, a pop-up window will appear with detailed characteristics of the item.
Hint. For a convenient comparison of the equipment to the one available on board, you can open the dialogue window over the store window "Ship" (S).

To buy an equipment in the store:
Click it with the left mouse button.
Note. The operation will not be possible, if you don't have enough money or free slots on your spaceship .

Note! All newly acquired equipment (except the hull) will be stored in the hold, and you will have to install it manually. Use the screen 'Ship' (S) for this. The new hull will be forwarded to your storage, you will be able to activate it from the window "Ship" (S) as well.
