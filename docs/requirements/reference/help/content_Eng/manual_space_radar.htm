Radar


The radar is an auxiliary window located in the upper right corner of the screen. With the help of the radar you can quickly move the camera to any point of the star current system. (The camera is the part, of space that the player sees in front of him on the screen at the current moment, its location is marked on the radar with a white rectangle.)

The center of the radar is taken by the current star, which the planets orbit around.

The bright green circle indicated the area of the radar action. The small precise images inside this area are the objects surrounding the player. The rest of the panel is dimmed. If the radar is not installed or broken, the entire panel is dimmed, and the player sees only himself and the star on the radar screen.


To move the radar to the object of your interest (ship or planet):
• Click a spot next to this object on the radar.

To return the camera to the player's ship:
• Press 'Center ship' (C) in the right bottom corner of the radar panel.
This button is useful, if the player lost his ship. :-)

To find out the amount of turns of some distant flight:

After you have set a point for a distant flight, on the radar you can see how many days it will take you. Hiver the mouse over your spaceship. The radar will display a dashed line colored red and green. Each part of the line symbolizes one turn.
Note. To find the distance within one turn you need to enable this mode in the game settings.
