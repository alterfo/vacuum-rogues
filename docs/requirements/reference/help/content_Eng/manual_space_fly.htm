Flight

To fly to a random point:

Option 1
Find a place, where you wish to fly to, and double-click it with the left mouse button.
If you indicated a planet or a station, you will automatically land on it. If you double-clicked a spaceship, your character will perform the action towards it, which is indicated in the game settings.

Option 2
1. Click any spot in the space, which you wish to fly to. The screen will display the trajectory of your ship's flight to this point with the indication of the amount of flight days. The green dots will mark the way, which you ship can cover within a day, orange - over a day.
2. Press the 'End of turn' button. Your ship will fly to the indicated destination.

To interrupt a multi-day flight:
Press the 'End of turn' button one more time. Your spaceship will stop at the end of the day.


Note. The 'Flight' order can be combined with the orders "Pick up item" and "Shoot ".
